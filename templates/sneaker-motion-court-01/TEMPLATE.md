# AIRPLAY / Sneaker Motion Court

An original cinematic lifestyle sneaker template built around a city route that laces itself. Desktop uses a diagonal triple-exposure crossing and oversized lace map; mobile becomes a thumb-led sneaker-box unboxing and focused route flow.

## Signature interaction

Visitors choose Court, Dance or All-day movement plus a colorway. The SVG lace path, fit zones, recommendation, product treatment and price update before a size-specific fictional first-step ticket can be issued.

## Original imagery

Five coordinated assets were created with the built-in OpenAI ImageGen workflow on 2026-08-21 and optimized locally to WebP:

- `airplay-hero.webp` — three-model ground-level crossing
- `airplay-motion.webp` — dance pivot on a wet night court
- `airplay-pair.webp` — original sneaker pair on painted boxes
- `airplay-sole.webp` — translucent outsole pivot macro
- `airplay-night.webp` — recurring cast in an after-dark crossing

The prompt set preserves one fictional asymmetric mesh-and-suede sneaker silhouette, recurring adult cast, direct-flash city lighting and logo-free surfaces. No third-party brand assets are used.

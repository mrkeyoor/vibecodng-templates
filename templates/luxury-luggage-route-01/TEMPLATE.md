# MERIDIAN / Interactive Route and Packing Folio

An original luxury-luggage campaign where destination, trip rhythm and duration redraw a schematic itinerary and repack a transparent case cross-section. The result resolves to a fictional perforated boarding folio.

Mobile becomes a vertical route and boarding document, not a reduced desktop map. Four coordinated campaign photographs are original generated assets stored locally as optimized WebP files. `BW_PALETTE`, keyboard use, 320px reflow, 200% text zoom and reduced motion are supported.

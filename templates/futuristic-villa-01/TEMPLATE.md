# Casa Nadir — Sectional Residence

An original future-villa template designed as a sectional promenade. A demand-rendered 3D house travels through arrival, living void, water court and roof coordinates by bounded scroll or explicit controls. Daylight is a working spatial input, not decorative ambience.

## Signature features

- Original code-native villa model with section cut, camera itinerary, water court and real daylight states.
- Explicit room coordinates mirror the scroll journey and preserve mobile/reduced-motion access.
- Solar section, material archive, coastal site ledger and private visit brief.
- Mobile pocket-section composition with thumb itinerary and inline confirmation.
- Two original locally shipped architectural campaign frames complement the code-native model; there is no stock photography, external runtime asset, generic listing grid or booking drawer.

All areas, location, completion, availability and project information are fictional sample content.

`villa-dusk.webp` and `villa-interior.webp` were created with OpenAI image generation for this template, manually inspected and optimized locally to WebP.

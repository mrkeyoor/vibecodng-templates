# Maison Fold — Pattern to Silhouette

An original couture template built around a pattern table that stitches. Scroll or explicit controls move four flat pattern pieces onto a dress form and resolve them into a finished silhouette. Runway timing, textile drape, seam mapping, look composition and a private fitting dossier continue the construction grammar.

All garment, collection, textile, atelier-time and fitting information is fictional sample content. `couture-runway.webp` and `couture-atelier.webp` are original OpenAI-generated editorial frames, manually inspected and optimized locally to WebP; the pattern assembly remains original SVG/CSS.

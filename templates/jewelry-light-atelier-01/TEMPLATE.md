# LUCENT / Light Reflection Atelier

An original contemporary-jewelry campaign centered on a functional light table. Metal, stone and environment change a loupe-scale ring, beam, reflection bloom and optical readout before resolving into a fictional gem-paper study.

Mobile uses a thumb-controlled jeweler loupe. Four coordinated macro, craft and model photographs are original generated assets stored locally as optimized WebP. `BW_PALETTE`, keyboard use, 320px reflow, 200% text zoom and reduced motion are supported.

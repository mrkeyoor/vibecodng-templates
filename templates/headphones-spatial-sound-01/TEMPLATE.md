# SONORA / Spatial Soundfield

An original premium-headphone campaign centered on a silent but functional spatial soundfield instrument. Room, stage width and sonic focus reposition sources, redraw the orbital radar and update acoustic readouts before resolving into a fictional listening passport.

Mobile uses a square thumb soundstage radar instead of shrinking the desktop composition. Four coordinated campaign images are original generated assets stored locally as optimized WebP files. `BW_PALETTE`, keyboard use, 320px reflow, 200% text zoom and reduced motion are supported.

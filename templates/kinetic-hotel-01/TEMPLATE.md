# EIRA — Cinematic Hotel

An original fictional cliffside retreat website designed as a cinematic editorial journey. Three coordinated architectural photographs move through a scroll-controlled portal before opening into rooms, dining, coast experiences, and a complete reservation enquiry.


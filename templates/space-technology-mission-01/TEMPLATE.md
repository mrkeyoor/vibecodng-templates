# Aperture Orbital — Mission Sequence

An original commercial-space template built around a mission that deploys. A demand-rendered spacecraft advances from ignition through separation and solar-array deployment to an imaging pass, using bounded scroll or explicit event controls. Coverage, payload bus, ground network and concept uplink continue the operational grammar.

All spacecraft performance, coverage, schedule, company and mission information is fictional illustrative content. `space-orbit.webp` and `mission-control.webp` are original OpenAI-generated campaign frames, manually inspected, optimized locally to WebP and shipped without external runtime assets.

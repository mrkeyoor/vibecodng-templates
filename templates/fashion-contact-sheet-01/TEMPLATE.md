# RIFT — Fashion Contact Sheet

An original cinematic React/Vite template for a fictional experimental ready-to-wear label. Its signature contact sheet develops from a darkroom exposure into a stateful styling docket and numbered, size-specific claim tag.

## What makes it distinct

- A restrained full-bleed campaign hero whose alternate exposure is revealed through scroll-linked photographic shutters
- An irregular three-take contact bed instead of a generic lookbook or product-card grid
- One live styling workstation where campaign frame, garment layers, fit, edition and price resolve together
- A material macro, night street proof and inline perforated hold tag with complete validation and confirmation
- A purpose-built mobile pocket-contact-strip composition rather than a collapsed desktop sticky film
- Semantic `BW_PALETTE` support, keyboard-operable controls, reduced motion and honest fictional product data

## Original campaign assets

The five local images below were generated specifically for this template with OpenAI image generation on 2026-08-21, visually inspected, and optimized to WebP. They depict a fictional, unbranded label and must not be represented as real inventory or an existing campaign.

- `public/rift-hero.webp` — master studio frame
- `public/rift-negative.webp` — same-model motion exposure
- `public/rift-duo.webp` — coordinated two-look collection proof
- `public/rift-street.webp` — same-model exterior motion proof
- `public/rift-material.webp` — silver ripstop, zipper, bartack and piping macro

The prompt set used a master-frame workflow: one original model and garment were generated first, then supplied as identity, construction, palette and lighting references for subsequent frames. Every prompt prohibited logos, typography, watermarks, copied campaigns and branded garments.

## Data and interaction note

All garment names, fit notes, editions, pricing, contact-frame metadata, inventory language and claim-tag confirmations are fictional demonstration content. No payment, inventory reservation or external request occurs.

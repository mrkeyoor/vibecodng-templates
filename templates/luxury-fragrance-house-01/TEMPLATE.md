# Orris Null — Olfactive Chamber

An original fragrance-house template built as a formula that distils. A demand-rendered optical-glass vessel moves from raw headspace to concentrate and sealed extrait; ingredient selection reorganises the visible formula, and atomisation is a short user-triggered event rather than a loop.

The supporting system includes a three-speed formula organ, evaporation curves, vessel section, blotter wallet and validated discovery dossier. `fragrance-campaign.webp` and `fragrance-ingredients.webp` are original OpenAI-generated editorial frames, manually inspected and optimized locally to WebP. All product, formula, batch and availability information is fictional sample content.
